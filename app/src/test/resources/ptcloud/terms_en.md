## Privacy

**Your content is not kept.** We never store a screenshot or any text you send or receive. They are
held only in memory, never written to storage, and we never use them to train anything.

**What leaves your device.** Only what you trigger. When you use a cloud feature, the app sends what
that feature works on: screenshots of your screen while cloud reading is on, text read from it,
anything you type to ask about it, the languages involved and the app version.

**Where it goes.** Screenshots go only to servers we operate. Text goes to AI providers to produce
the answer you asked for. Requests pass through our hosting and network providers. We are based in
the United States. Your data is processed there and in other countries where we or our providers
operate.

**What we keep.** A record of each request (counts, sizes, timings, how it was answered and what it
cost, the app version and your country) for 90 days, and monthly totals per account after that;
your email address, the name our payment service passes on, payment references, your keys in
hashed form, and which version of these terms you accepted, when and in which language. We use
these to provide and meter the service, prevent abuse and bill you, and for nothing else. We do not
sell your data.

**Deletion.** Ask at support@playtranslate.com and we delete your account: your email, name, keys,
membership and trial. The request records above stay, under an account id that no longer links to
anyone, and payment records stay as long as accounting requires.
